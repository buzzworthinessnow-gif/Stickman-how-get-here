### Image prompt starter

2D cinematic illustrated historical scene, [date and place]. [Stable stickman character description] [specific pose/action] with [era-correct featured object and materials] in [detailed historically appropriate setting]. Foreground [action], midground [props], background [architecture/geography]. Camera [angle and framing]; lighting [direction, time, mood]; palette [colors]; tactile storybook ink-and-paper finish. Keep all people as 2D stickmen, preserve object master and continuity, accurate mechanism, clear silhouette, leave negative space for date label. [16:9 or 9:16].
